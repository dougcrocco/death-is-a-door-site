Death Is a Door

A group exhibition curated by Doug Crocco
DatesNovember 12 – [closing date], 2026 OpeningThursday, November 12, 6–9 pm LocationCC Jasper, 131 Chrystie Street, New York, NY 10002

New York, NY — CC Jasper is pleased to present Death Is a Door, a group exhibition curated by Doug Crocco of painting, sculpture, photography and moving image made at the threshold between this world and whatever lies past it. The exhibition opens Thursday, November 12, 2026, with a reception from 6 to 9 pm.

A door is not an ending. It is the last architecture of the living — a frame we build around the one passage we cannot see through. Death Is a Door gathers work that lingers in the room between two worlds rather than choosing a side.

Every culture builds a passage for the dead — a ferry, a gate, a veil, a flight of stairs into water. The passage is never the destination. It is the interval: the held breath between what was and what comes after. The works in this exhibition exist inside that interval. Their subjects are not death itself but its thresholds — a locked door, a boat mid-crossing, flowers already turning, fruit at the edge of rot. Beauty and decay share the same frame, because at the door they are the same event.

To stand before these works is to stand at the threshold — and to be asked, gently, to look through.
