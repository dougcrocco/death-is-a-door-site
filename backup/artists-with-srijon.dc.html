Artists — Death Is a Door
The Artists

Many practices, one threshold

Photography · I

Doug Crocco

Artist and curator
Lives and works in Los Angeles, CA

Artist and curator. Crocco founded Big Pictures Los Angeles in 2014 and ran the online project Cult2vader.com from 2006 to 2016. He has shown with ACME and Happy Lion Gallery, Los Angeles. MFA, Claremont Graduate University.

Moving Image · II

unitedfutures

Studio of Michael “Caco” Peguero
Lives and works in Los Angeles, CA

The studio of digital media artist Michael “Caco” Peguero, working across augmented and virtual reality, generative systems and projection mapping. He lectures at UCLA Architecture and Urban Design and has worked on projects by Pierre Huyghe, Hito Steyerl and Ian Cheng. M.Arch, Harvard GSD.

Painting · III

Ellen R Hanson

Midwest-born painter
Lives and works in Mexico City

Hanson paints on textiles stretched over bars, probing how femininity is performed. Solo shows at Smart Objects, Los Angeles, and NARANJO 141, Mexico City. MFA, Virginia Commonwealth University.

Painting · IV

Benjamin Cowan

b. 1985, Ann Arbor, MI
Lives and works in Brooklyn, NY

Cowan paints windows and altarpieces that echo Gothic art, often built around fragments of Old Master paintings. He has shown at Deanna Evans Projects and The FLAG Art Foundation, New York. MFA, Indiana University.

Painting · V, XI

Meredith Sellers

b. 1988, Baltimore, MD
Lives and works in Philadelphia, PA

Sellers’ paintings borrow images from art history, stock photography, advertising and the news to examine power and violence. She has shown at Fragment, New York, and Workplace, London. MFA, University of Pennsylvania.

Painting · VI

Henry Swanson

b. 1993, Dallas, TX
Lives and works in Brooklyn, NY

Swanson makes large oil paintings and mixed-media works drawing on cartoons, comics and his childhood in Dallas. Recent solo shows at Plan X, Milan, and Anthony Gallery, Chicago. BFA, Rhode Island School of Design.

Painting · VII

Aramis Gutierrez

b. 1975, Pittsburgh, PA
Lives and works in Miami, FL

Gutierrez paints large canvases in the idiom of classical painting, around love, untimely death and corruption. Co-founder of the Miami spaces Noguchi Breton and Tile Blush; solo shows at David Castillo and Big Pictures. BFA, Cooper Union.

Sculpture · VIII

L

1984–2026
Lived and worked in Los Angeles, CA

Artist and spiritual practitioner, earlier known as Jason Metcalf and as Lazaros. He made spells — objects sealed in mineral oil inside glass. Solo shows at Martos Gallery, New York; JOAN, Los Angeles; and And Now, Dallas; later shown at the Getty Center and Documenta 15.

Painting · IX, XV

Charlie Goering

b. 1993, Fort Mitchell, KY
Lives and works in Cincinnati, OH

Goering works across painting, drawing and collage, pairing unrelated objects and images. Recipient of an Elizabeth Greenshields Foundation grant. BFA, Laguna College of Art and Design.

Painting · X

Tom DeLaney

b. 1977, Tallahassee, FL
Lives and works in New York, NY

DeLaney’s paintings and drawings draw on imagery from television, film and video, as meditations on void, self and loss. He also plays music in New York as Dr. Nowww. BFA, Florida State University.

Painting · XII

Pandele Radu

b. 1993, Arad, Romania
Lives and works in Bucharest, Romania

Radu works across painting, murals, ceramics and digital media, often using 3D modelling, and is a member of the mural collective Sweet Damage Crew. He has shown at the National Museum of Contemporary Art, Bucharest.

Sculpture · XIII

Megan Lindeman

Transdisciplinary artist
Lives and works in [city]

Lindeman’s work joins the tangible with the intangible, drawing on neuroscience, linguistics and world history. She teaches at Norco College. BFA, Rhode Island School of Design; studied at Claremont Graduate University.

Sculpture · XIV

Kenny Curwood

Artist and filmmaker
Lives and works in Brooklyn, NY

Curwood works in film, animation and sculpture, shooting frame by frame on repurposed cameras and processing by hand; he also makes brass and solder sculptures. He has shown at Anthology Film Archives, Canada Gallery and Microscope Gallery, New York. BFA, School of Visual Arts.

Painting · XVI

Srijon Chowdhury

b. 1987, Dhaka, Bangladesh
Lives and works in Portland, OR

Chowdhury’s narrative oil paintings draw on Islamic architecture, William Blake, tarot and the Western occult. First museum solo show at the Frye Art Museum, 2022; represented by P·P·O·W, New York, and Ciaccia Levi, Paris. MFA, Otis College of Art and Design.
