Death Is a Door · Event

Silverlake Scent Club
with Megan Lindeman

A scent created for Death Is a Door
DateSaturday, November 14, 2026 TimeAfternoon · time to be announced LocationCC Jasper, 131 Chrystie Street, New York, NY 10002

New York, NY — On the first Saturday afternoon of Death Is a Door, artist Megan Lindeman brings her Silverlake Scent Club to CC Jasper to present a fragrance made in response to the exhibition.

Founded by Lindeman in Los Angeles in 2025, the Silverlake Scent Club gathers monthly to explore fragrance as both material and memory — smelled, discussed and shared in common. For this edition, the club meets in the gallery, among the works, around a scent composed for the show’s threshold between this world and whatever lies past it.

Lindeman’s practice links paint, pigment and the body’s chemistry of bonding and trust. Her painting in the exhibition, New eNightened Era, Coming Soon (2026), hangs alongside the evening’s scent.

About the artist

Megan Lindeman is a Los Angeles–based artist and professor. She mixes paint and pigment with oxytocin, the hormone involved in bonding and trust, and works across painting, works on paper, books and scent.
