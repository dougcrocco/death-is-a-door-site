Death Is a Door · Event

Artist Talk:
unitedfutures

AI, art and technology
DateSunday, November 15, 2026 TimeAfternoon · time to be announced LocationCC Jasper, 131 Chrystie Street, New York, NY 10002

New York, NY — On the first Sunday afternoon of Death Is a Door, unitedfutures gives a talk on AI, art and technology — how machine tools are changing the way images are made, and what remains the artist’s hand. He will discuss The Isle of the Dead 2026, his moving-image work in the exhibition, as a case in point.

Peguero comes to the subject from inside the tools. Trained as an architect at the Harvard Graduate School of Design, he works across generative systems, augmented and virtual reality and projection mapping, and has worked on projects by Pierre Huyghe, Hito Steyerl and Ian Cheng — artists central to how simulation and machine intelligence entered contemporary art. He lectures at UCLA Architecture and Urban Design, has taught at Woodbury University, and was a 2022–23 CultureHub resident in Los Angeles.

The talk takes place in the gallery, among the works, and is followed by an informal conversation. Free and open to all.

About the artist

unitedfutures is the Los Angeles studio of digital media artist and educator Michael “Caco” Peguero (M.Arch, Harvard GSD), working at the intersection of visual art, new media and immersive environments.
