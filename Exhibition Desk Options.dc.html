5Saved in this browser.
1
Log buttonSits next to + Add. The number is how many changes have been recorded. Click it to open the log.
2
WhenDate and time of the change, newest at the top.
3
ActionWhat happened, colour-coded:
AddedYou saved a new item with + Add
DeletedYou clicked ✕ on a card
MovedYou dragged a card to another urgency column
RestoredYou clicked Restore deleted
ResetYou clicked Reset order & urgency
4
ItemThe area, the item and its details. Moves show the new column, adds show the urgency it was given.
5
Where it livesKept in this browser, up to the last 500 changes. No clear button, so nothing is lost by accident. It doesn't sync to other computers.
